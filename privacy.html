Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Purpose and scope

We collect only information you choose to submit and limited analytics data when consent is granted. Contact details are used to respond to enquiries and are not sold. You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing us.

This policy applies to Aromatic Food Corner, its recipe journal, recipe index, contact page and related editorial pages. It explains the conditions that apply when you browse, submit an enquiry, use a recipe idea or make a privacy choice.

Information and site use

We use limited technical information to operate and secure the site. Information typed into the contact form is provided voluntarily and should not include payment details, passwords, medical records or other highly sensitive data. We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, legal obligations and dispute prevention.

Third-party services and links

Pages may link to independent websites for additional context. Their content, availability and privacy practices are outside our control. Google Analytics is configured with Consent Mode and optional analytics storage remains denied unless a visitor accepts it through the privacy notice.

Food, allergy and safety responsibility

Recipe stories are flexible editorial examples. Ingredients, cooking times and storage needs vary. Readers must verify allergens, dietary suitability, safe internal temperatures, local public-health guidance and manufacturer instructions before preparing or serving food.

Intellectual property and acceptable use

Original written content, page design and branded presentation are protected by applicable intellectual-property rules. Personal reading and ordinary linking are welcome. Automated scraping, misleading republication, removal of attribution, harmful interference and commercial reuse without written permission are not permitted.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, object to certain processing or withdraw consent. You can also clear browser storage and change analytics preferences. We may need limited verification before completing a privacy request.

Updates and governing principles

We may revise this page when the website, services or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be presented clearly, and continued use after an update means the current terms apply to future visits.

Additional detail about using this journal

Aromatic Food Corner is an independent editorial resource. Its pages are prepared for general reading and practical inspiration, but every kitchen, ingredient and reader is different. Ingredient brands, appliances, altitude, humidity, experience and serving needs can change a result. Readers remain responsible for checking current packaging, equipment instructions, safe preparation methods and the suitability of a recipe for everyone who may eat it. Where this page and a mandatory legal right conflict, the mandatory right takes priority.

Information, records and reasonable safeguards

Website systems may process limited technical records such as network address, browser type, request time, requested page and error details to deliver pages, diagnose failures and protect the service from misuse. Access should be limited to people and providers with a practical need. We use proportionate organisational and technical safeguards, but no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Visitors should not send passwords, full payment details, health records or identity documents through general forms or ordinary email.

Consent, preferences and browser controls

Optional audience measurement is intended to reveal broad patterns such as popular pages and device categories, not to create individual cooking profiles. Analytics storage is denied by default through Consent Mode unless a visitor accepts. A preference may be stored in the browser so the notice does not appear on every page. Visitors can clear site data, block cookies or use private browsing controls; doing so can reset the saved choice and cause the notice to return.

Food safety, allergens and individual needs

Ingredient lists may not identify every source of an allergen, and substitutions can introduce new risks. Packaging and manufacturing practices can change without notice. Anyone managing allergies, intolerances, pregnancy-related restrictions, religious requirements or a medically prescribed diet must verify current labels and seek qualified guidance where appropriate. Cooking times are estimates. Follow current public-health advice for cleanliness, separation, temperature, cooling, refrigeration, reheating and leftovers, and use a reliable thermometer where guidance recommends one.

Changes, availability and external resources

We may correct errors, revise recipes, update links, reorganise collections or withdraw material. Maintenance, provider changes and events outside reasonable control may interrupt access. External links are offered for context and convenience; third-party content, products, prices, privacy practices and availability remain outside our control. Inclusion does not guarantee endorsement. Readers should independently confirm information whenever safety, health, cost, legal obligations or an important personal decision is involved.

Requests and contact expectations

Questions may be sent to hello@aromaticfoodcorner.com. Please identify the relevant page and describe the issue clearly without including unnecessary sensitive information. Depending on applicable law, a person may be entitled to request access, correction or deletion of personal data, restrict certain processing, object or withdraw consent. Rights can have lawful exceptions, and reasonable identity verification may be required so information is not disclosed to the wrong person. We aim to acknowledge legitimate requests promptly and explain any action or limitation in plain language.
